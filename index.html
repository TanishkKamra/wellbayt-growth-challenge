Part A. Three Ways to Start Genuine Conversations

Replacing generic outreach with timing-driven triggers, calendar relevance, and pre-researched value.

1. Moment Radar: Reach Companies at the Moment of Change

Wellbeing budgets move when leadership, offices, or headcount shift. Automation watches public news for triggers, ranks them by freshness, and drafts openers citing the real event.

Real Examples (From Recorded Run):
Signal Opened a regional HQ in Dubai (6 days ago).
Why Target Transport operator with shift-based and frontline staff. A new regional team is forming, so culture and retention are set early.
How I Would Approach Note to the regional HR/People lead: congratulate on the HQ, offer a short onsite movement and wellbeing session for the first cohort, designed around shift patterns.
Signal Opened a 7,200 sqm Dubai South hub (about 30 days ago).
Why Target A logistics and distribution site means a large on-feet workforce with physical strain and fatigue, a natural fit for movement and ergonomics workshops.
How I Would Approach Approach the hub's HR/operations lead with a practical "healthy shift" pilot: short stretch breaks, posture workshop, a team step challenge. Offer to run a single pilot day.
Signal Launched UAE operations and a Dubai office (about 20 days ago).
Why Target They sell to HR leaders, so they know the value of wellbeing. A new UAE team is being built and onboarded now.
How I Would Approach Offer an onboarding-week wellbeing activation and a movement challenge that builds the new Dubai team's bonds. Position it as a culture launch, not a vendor pitch.

2. Calendar-Led Challenges

Instead of pitching generic workshops, offer a ready-made participation moment tied to key UAE dates HR already plans for: World Mental Health Day (10 October), Ramadan, the annual Dubai fitness season, new-year budget cycles, and UAE working-week transitions.

Execution: Automation maps each target company's industry and headcount to the most compelling seasonal date, generating a clean one-page "company challenge" HR can join in a single click.

3. Give-First Wellbeing Snapshot

For top-tier accounts, assemble a pre-researched one-page snapshot using public data only, sent as a gift showing homework before asking for anything.

Execution: Synthesises public intelligence: headcount, sites, industry shift patterns, and three tailored programme recommendations. About 10 high-value snapshots dispatched each week, each verified by hand.
Why this beats cold email: Every communication cites a real event, a real calendar date, or a real operational need. High reply rates stem from relevance, not volume, and every touchpoint primes the CRM with context for the discovery meeting.

Honest Limits & Human Verification

Automation surfaces opportunities; human judgment ensures relevance, tone, and brand safety.

Rule-Based Extraction

Company extraction uses regex heuristics from news titles. A human reviews every lead before outreach to prevent misidentified entities.

Public News Coverage

Public news feeds capture prominent announcements, but miss subtle signals like internal promotions. The next iteration adds targeted role tracking.

Zero Blind Outreach

Nothing is dispatched automatically. The radar produces a clean review queue for Tanishk to verify, enrich, and approve individually.

Ethical Testing

No real prospects were contacted during this exercise. All signals are sourced from public 2026 press reporting across the UAE.
